Dapur Kilat — an original fast-paced cooking game. Take orders from the board, grab ingredients from the counter, chop them on the board, grill the patties, assemble each dish on the plate, and serve it at the window before the customer walks away. Playable with keyboard and mouse in any desktop browser, no network needed.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

## How to play

- **WASD / arrow keys** — move the chef; **click a station** to walk to it.
- **E** — use a station (hold E to chop); **1–5** — pick an ingredient bin; **P** — pause.
- Tomatoes and lettuce must be chopped, patties must be grilled (don't let them burn!), buns and cheese go straight on the plate.
- Serve a finished plate at the serving window. Survive the whole lunch rush — three lost customers and the kitchen closes.
